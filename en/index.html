Core flow

How It Works

1. Take a photo

Capture each storybook page with the camera. LazyDaddy recognizes the text automatically.

2. Save the book

Review the extracted text, correct recognition mistakes, and save the pages as one book.

3. Listen together

Play the saved book and let TTS read it aloud while you sit beside your child.

Features

Main Features

Camera capture and OCR

On-device Google ML Kit text recognition extracts Korean and English text from book pages.

AI voice reading

Natural voices are available through Google Cloud TTS, with Android built-in TTS as an option.

Multiple speakers

Dialogue can be analyzed so narrator and character voices can be configured separately.

Your local bookshelf

Saved storybooks stay on the device, with no account or cloud sync required.

Retake and edit pages

Retake a page or edit recognized text whenever OCR needs a little help.

English storybook support

English OCR and voices help children hear storybooks with clear pronunciation.

Personalized stories

Edit recognized text to add your child's name or adapt a book into your family's own story.

No sign-up required

Start using the app without login, subscription, or a cloud account.

Privacy and Data Safety

  • The camera is used only to capture book pages, and captured images are not uploaded by the app.
  • OCR processing runs on the device.
  • Saved storybook text is stored locally on the device.
  • Book text is sent to Google only when Google Cloud TTS is used to generate speech.
  • AdMob ads appear after save or edit transitions, not during the reading experience.
  • Collected personal information is not sold to third parties.

Read the privacy policy for more details.
